This digital focusses on assessing the quality of air across four cities (namely Chicago, Sacremento, Bangalore and New Delhi) through digital twin. The digital twin web app consists of three pages.

1. Data Coverage Summary 
This page gives information on the daily coverage at each location of the selected city. 
It displays the pollutants available at given location within a city. The data is measured at 1 hour frequency when it is available.
The data coverage is indicated by the color. 100% data corresponds to dark red and 0% corresponds to yellow.
To collect the data I studied the API docs of OpenAQ Inc at (https://docs.openaq.org/).
Their generous API rate limits was sufficient for my project. So a big thanks to them.


2. Sensor Locations and Raw data
This page helps visualize the sensor locations in the selected city on the left panel.
The right panel helps in visualizing the pollutant levels with time at selected location at the top.
while the bottom figure on the right panel shows how the pollutant at the given location compares with the pollutants at the rest of the location.
One could discern how the pollutant level at the selected location compares with other locations in the city.

3. Forecasting of Average Values
This pages is about forecasting the average value of the pollutant at a given location. The forecast is done a Prophet. 
The raw data is shown as black dots. The trend in the data is displayed as orange line, while the forecast in the future is shown in blue line.
The blue shades indicate 95% confidence interval.
